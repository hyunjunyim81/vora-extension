VORA iPhone Privacy Policy

2026-11-25

1. Scope and contact

This policy applies to the VORA iPhone app provided by MINJIYUN. Contact mgfrind@gmail.com with questions. VORA does not require an app account or login. This policy distinguishes on-device processing from processing by advertising, payment, email and web-hosting providers.

2. Photos, videos and audio

The camera is used to take photos and record videos. The microphone is used when video audio is enabled. Captured and edited results are kept in app-local storage and saved to Apple Photos. The VORA gallery manages items saved by the app; it does not browse your entire Photos library. Folders are app-local categories. VORA has no feature that uploads captured photos, videos or audio to a developer-operated server.

3. Face AR and mosaics

Face AR uses MediaPipe and face mosaics use Apple Vision for on-device processing. Face positions and landmarks support selected effects and mosaics, rather than identifying a person. VORA does not send face images or landmarks to a developer server or maintain a face-profile database. Photos and videos you save may contain the selected effects or mosaic results.

4. Permissions

Camera, microphone and Photos add/read-write access are requested for the corresponding features. Permission to add a new result is separate from access needed to delete or reconcile saved items. You can change permissions in iPhone Settings. Denied access can prevent the corresponding feature or save operation from completing. Granting an operating-system permission is not blanket consent to advertising or other external processing.

5. Settings and local records

The app keeps settings such as theme, selected filters and AR effects, camera options, logical folders, media records and advertising display dates in local storage. It also keeps records to avoid duplicate publication to Photos and recover failed or uncertain save operations. Operating-system backups and photo synchronization may keep separate copies according to the services and settings you use.

6. Advertising and consent

VORA uses Google AdMob and User Messaging Platform. Advertising and consent services may process device or app identifiers, IP addresses and approximate location, ad views and interactions, and performance or error diagnostics for advertising, measurement, analytics and service operation. Processing depends on SDK behavior, consent and device settings. Local media processing does not mean all SDK information stays on the device. Please also review Google’s privacy and advertising information.

7. Premium purchases

Premium is a one-time, non-consumable Apple in-app purchase. Apple and the App Store process payment; VORA does not receive your card number or payment password. The app uses verified StoreKit transactions, current entitlements and revocation status to remove ads, enable detailed filter controls and restore purchases. AR, basic capture and filter selection do not require Premium. Apple policies govern purchase history, retention and refunds.

8. Sharing and deletion

Sharing sends media to an app or service you choose; subsequent processing follows that recipient’s policies. App-local copies and copies saved to Photos are distinct. Deleting a Photos copy may require system access and confirmation. Removing VORA does not necessarily remove copies in Photos, external recipients or operating-system backups.

9. Retention and recovery

Local settings, records and media support app features and save recovery and may be removed through deletion or app removal. When publication to Photos is uncertain, VORA reconciles saved records instead of automatically publishing again. VORA does not set a single retention period or remotely delete data held by Photos, backup services, sharing recipients or advertising and payment providers. Support emails may remain as needed to handle an inquiry; do not send unnecessary face images or sensitive attachments.

10. Your choices and rights

You can adjust iPhone permissions and photo/backup settings and use media deletion, sharing and purchase restoration. Where required, advertising privacy options in Settings let you review consent choices. Premium removes in-app advertising; it does not automatically erase information already lawfully processed by another provider. Contact the public email for applicable privacy rights or inquiries involving a child or legal representative.

11. Policy hosting and external providers

These static pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. VORA adds no login, analytics or advertising scripts to them. GitHub may process connection information while hosting the pages. Apple, Google, recipients, email providers and hosting providers apply their own security, international-processing and retention practices. The links below explain those providers’ policies.

12. Updates and contact

This policy may change with app features, external services or applicable requirements. The displayed revision date identifies the current version. Material changes follow notice and consent procedures required by applicable law. App and privacy contact: mgfrind@gmail.com.
