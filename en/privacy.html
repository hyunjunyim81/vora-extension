1. Who provides VORA

The app is provided by the developer listed as MINJIYUN on Google Play. For questions or privacy requests concerning information handled by the developer, contact mgfrind@gmail.com. Google and GitHub also handle information through their services under their own privacy notices, linked below.

2. Photos, videos and audio

Camera images, selected photos, recorded video and microphone audio are processed on the device to provide capture, filters, editing and saving. Microphone audio is used for recording when you enable sound and grant permission. VORA does not upload this media to a developer-operated server for these features. Sharing media to another app or enabling an external photo-backup service is a separate action governed by that service.

3. Face effects and automatic face mosaic

Face AR processes face landmarks and tracking information on the device to place visual effects. Automatic face mosaic also detects faces on the device. These features do not identify who a person is or build a developer-held face-identity database. Working face coordinates are used temporarily during processing; saved photos or videos can contain the visible effect or mosaic. VORA does not send face images or landmark results to a developer-operated server for these functions.

4. Permissions

Camera permission supports capture, microphone permission supports optional video audio, and image/media access supports browsing and editing. On older supported Android versions, storage-read permission is used instead of the newer image permission. You can manage permissions in Android settings, which can disable dependent features. VORA does not request device-location permission, but an advertising provider may infer approximate location from an IP address.

5. Settings, purchases and support messages

The app stores preferences such as theme, camera and filter settings, recording options, selected AR effect settings and a Premium-status cache locally. Google Play processes payments and supplies purchase identifiers and status used to provide or restore Premium; VORA does not directly receive your full payment-card number. If you email support, the address and information you choose to send are handled to answer the request and meet applicable obligations. Avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information.

6. Advertising and other providers

Google AdMob may handle IP addresses, device or advertising identifiers, app/ad interactions and diagnostics for advertising, measurement and abuse prevention. UMP manages applicable privacy choices. ML Kit processes face-detection input and results on the device, but may contact Google for updates or compatibility information and send performance or usage metrics. Google Play handles purchase data. These policy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, whose host may process request information such as IP addresses and browser details. VORA adds no analytics script to these pages. See the providers' policies below.

7. Your choices and processing purposes

Local processing provides the features you request. Purchase and support information is used to deliver the purchase, answer you and meet applicable legal obligations. Where consent is required for advertising or related processing, the relevant consent choices are presented; a device permission is not a blanket consent to unrelated processing. When available, use the app's Privacy Options to review choices. Android advertising settings and Google's controls provide further options. Premium suppresses app ads but does not remove purchase or support processing. Where applicable, legal bases are performance of the requested service or purchase, legitimate interests in answering and securing support, legal obligations, and consent where required.

8. Storage, deletion and backups

Saved photos and videos are stored in shared device media storage and may remain after uninstalling VORA. Delete them through the app or your gallery, and separately manage any shared or backed-up copies. Internal settings and caches can be removed using Android app-data controls or uninstalling; Android backup or device-transfer settings may restore some app data. Support information is retained only as needed for the request and applicable obligations. Google Play purchase records and third-party records follow those providers' retention rules.

9. Privacy rights and international processing

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability or withdrawal of consent, and complain to a competent privacy authority. Contact the developer for information the developer handles; media remaining only on your device cannot be retrieved or deleted remotely by the developer. Requests about Google or GitHub records may also need to be addressed to that provider. Their services can process information in other countries under their stated safeguards and policies.

10. Security and family enquiries

VORA uses Android's permissions and app-storage protections for local information. Advertising, payment, email and hosting providers apply their own transport and security measures. No device or service can be guaranteed completely secure. Contact mgfrind@gmail.com if you suspect misuse of information or need help with a child's or guardian's privacy request; do not include unnecessary face images or other sensitive attachments.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app features, providers or legal requirements change. The date above identifies the current version. Material changes will be communicated, and any additional notice or consent will be provided, where required by applicable law. Contact mgfrind@gmail.com with questions.
